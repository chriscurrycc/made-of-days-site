Made of Days

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026 · 简体中文

Made of Days has no servers and no account. Everything you record stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account, which only you can read. Nothing is ever sent to us, and there are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

Made of Days stores the following on your device:

  • The activities and dimensions you create, including their names, icons and colors
  • Your check-ins: the date, the time, and the duration where applicable
  • Journal text you write, and photos you attach
  • Reminders you set
  • Your settings and preferences

Apple Health

If you choose to use automatic check-ins, Made of Days reads the following from Apple Health, and only the types belonging to the activities you set up:

Steps · Walking + Running Distance · Flights Climbed · Active Energy · Exercise Minutes · Stand Hours · Sleep · Mindful Minutes · Water · Caffeine · Body Mass · Blood Pressure · Blood Glucose · Workouts

  • Read only. The app never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Health data is read on your device, used only to decide whether an activity counts as checked in, and never leaves your device.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or similar services, and is never shared with or sold to any third party.
  • You can revoke access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Photos

Photos you attach to an entry are copied into the app's own storage on your device. Text in photos is recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework, so that you can search your journal by something written in a picture. Photos and the recognized text are never uploaded to us.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your records are synced through your own private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and counts toward your iCloud storage. We have no access to it, and no ability to read it. Turning sync off stops further syncing; data already in your iCloud account can be removed from Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a small local diagnostic log (recent events such as "a record was saved", counts, and dates) to help investigate problems. It never contains your journal text, activity names, photos or health values. It is never transmitted anywhere. You may choose to export a diagnostics bundle yourself and send it to us; if you do, only what you send is what we receive.

Made of Days also uses Apple's MetricKit, which delivers crash and performance reports from the system to the app on the next launch. These stay on your device unless you export them.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales reports.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, no sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of your data, because we never have it

Children

Made of Days is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a record removes it and its photos from your device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored locally. If you used iCloud sync, you can remove the app's iCloud data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be posted at this address.

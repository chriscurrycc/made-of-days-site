Reporting a problem

If something went wrong, the app can put together everything I need: open Settings › About › Send Log, which hands the report straight to the share sheet — mail it to the address above. It contains recent events, counts and dates, and which build you are running; never your journal text, activity names, photos or health values.

Your data

Everything you record stays on your device, and on your own iCloud account if you turn on sync. The privacy policy has the details.

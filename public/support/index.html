Reporting a problem

If something goes wrong, open Settings › About and tap Send Log to send me the app's log. It helps me find the cause faster, and it contains none of your check-in data. A line or two about what you were doing when it happened, like which screen you were on, makes it much easier to track down.

Made of Days Pro

Buying, restoring and redeeming a code all happen on one page: Settings › Made of Days Pro. If Pro doesn't show up on a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases there.

To cancel the yearly subscription, tap Manage Subscription on that page, or go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Pro stays on until the current period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything you record stays on your device. With Made of Days Pro, it also syncs through your own private iCloud account. The privacy policy has the details.
